# Requirements Checklist

- [ ] Is the application purpose clear?
- [ ] Are the functional requirements simple and testable?
- [ ] Is the page content defined clearly enough?
- [ ] Is the technical scope limited to JSP + Maven + Jenkinsfile?
- [ ] Are out-of-scope items documented?
- [ ] Are acceptance criteria measurable?
- [ ] Can the app be completed within the workshop duration?
- [ ] Does the build output have a clear success condition?
